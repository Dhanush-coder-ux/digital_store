Required by the SPDI Rules, which treat passwords as sensitive personal information:

- Hash with **bcrypt (cost ≥ 12)** or **Argon2id**. Never MD5, SHA-1 or plain SHA-256.
- Never log, email or display a password — including in error messages and crash reports.
- On reset, send a **single-use, time-limited token**, never the password itself.
- Rate-limit login attempts and lock after repeated failures.
- Store OTPs hashed with a short expiry, and invalidate on use.
